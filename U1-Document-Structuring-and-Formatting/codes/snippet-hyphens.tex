The hyphen is used for compound words, such as a well-known author, a so-called expert.

The en-dash is used for ranges and linking concepts/names pages 10--20 or pages 30\textendash 50, the years 1990--2000 or 1850\textendash 1890, the Boyle--Mariotte law or Bose\textendash Einstein condensate.

The em-dash is used for parenthetical remarks or shifts in thought \LaTeX---a typesetting system---is powerful or Python\textemdash a high-level language\textemdash is versatile.
